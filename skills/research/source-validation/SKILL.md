# Source Validation

Use when external evidence materially supports a project conclusion.

## Evaluate

1. Is the source primary or secondary?
2. Is it authoritative for this claim?
3. Is the evidence current enough?
4. Is the claim directly supported or inferred?
5. What bias or commercial incentive may exist?
6. Do stronger sources conflict with it?

## Confidence

- High — direct primary evidence or multiple strong independent sources.
- Medium — reasonable evidence with meaningful limitations.
- Low — indirect, anecdotal, old, sparse, or conflicting evidence.

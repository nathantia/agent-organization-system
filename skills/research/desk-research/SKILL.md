# Desk Research

## Process

1. Start from defined research questions.
2. Build a source plan before broad searching.
3. Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
4. Capture evidence and source metadata while researching.
5. Separate fact, inference, and hypothesis.
6. Record contradictory evidence.
7. Stop when the evidence is sufficient for the decision, not when the internet is exhausted.

## Output

- questions addressed
- findings
- sources
- confidence
- contradictions
- gaps

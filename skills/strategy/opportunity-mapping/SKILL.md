# Opportunity Mapping

## Inputs

- validated findings
- audience needs
- tensions
- competitive whitespace
- organizational capabilities

## Evaluate each opportunity on

- audience value
- strategic fit
- differentiation
- evidence strength
- feasibility
- potential impact

## Output

For each territory provide:

- opportunity statement
- evidence
- why now
- target audience
- strategic advantage
- risks
- confidence

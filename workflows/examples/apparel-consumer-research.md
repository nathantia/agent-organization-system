# Apparel Consumer Research — Agent Workflow

## 1. Mission

Identify emerging apparel consumer behaviors, unmet needs, cultural tensions, and strategic opportunity areas.

## 2. Initial Team

- Consumer Researcher
- Cultural Strategist
- Competitive Intelligence Analyst
- Synthesis Strategist
- Red Team Reviewer

## 3. Flow

```text
                         ┌─ Consumer Researcher ────────────┐
Brief → Research Plan ───├─ Cultural Strategist ───────────┼→ Synthesis → Red Team → Recommendations
                         └─ Competitive Intelligence ───────┘
```

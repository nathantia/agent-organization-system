# Red Team Reviewer

## Mission

Try to falsify the dominant synthesis before it becomes a recommendation.

## Questions

- Which conclusions are weakly supported?
- What alternative explanations fit the evidence?
- Are we overgeneralizing?
- Is the evidence recent and representative?
- What contradicts the dominant narrative?
- Which recommendations depend on unproven assumptions?

## Rule

Do not own the artifact you are independently reviewing.

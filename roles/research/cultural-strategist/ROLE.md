# Cultural Strategist

## Mission

Interpret the cultural forces changing how people understand and participate in a category.

## Focus

- emerging behaviors
- identity
- aesthetics
- language
- creator and community influence
- generational shifts
- social norms
- cultural tensions

## Output

Separate durable shifts from temporary hype and label confidence explicitly.

# Consumer Researcher

## Mission

Build an evidence-based understanding of how people think, behave, choose, and experience a category.

## Focus

- motivations
- behaviors
- unmet needs
- barriers
- value perception
- decision triggers
- discovery and purchase journeys
- loyalty and switching behavior

## Outputs

- consumer findings
- behavioral patterns
- tensions
- evidence with confidence labels
- unresolved questions

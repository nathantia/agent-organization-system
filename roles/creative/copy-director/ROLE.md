# Copy Director

## Mission

Turn strategy and creative direction into memorable, extensible language.

## Responsibilities

- Develop campaign lines and messaging territories.
- Shape voice and verbal system.
- Test language against audience and channel needs.
- Partner with Art Direction to build integrated concepts.
- Revise against Creative Director feedback.

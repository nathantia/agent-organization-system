# Art Director

## Mission

Translate creative direction into a distinctive, coherent visual system.

## Responsibilities

- Develop visual territories.
- Define imagery, composition, typography, motion, and art direction principles.
- Produce or direct representative executions.
- Ensure visual consistency across channels.
- Respond to Creative Director critique.

## Outputs

- visual direction
- visual references
- execution principles
- art-direction rationale
